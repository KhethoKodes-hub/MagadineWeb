Who We Are

BLACK
WOMAN
OWNED &
LED

Founded in 2016 in the Thaba Chweu Municipality, Magadine Business Enterprise (Pty) Ltd was built from the ground up with purpose. We are a 100% Black Woman Owned business serving the mining and industrial heartland of Limpopo.

Miss Marumo Mkhonto leads as sole director (51%), partnering with Ms. Mahlatse Lekwadu — a disabled black woman — who holds 49%. Our leadership reflects the diversity and resilience of the communities we serve.
